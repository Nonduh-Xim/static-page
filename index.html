Impact of Modelling in My Growth

Personal Development

Watching fashion shows and noting the advice given by judges helps me learn and improve. Practice makes perfect. Making time to walk every day helps me become better.

Social Development

Modelling has helped me interact with people more easily. Although I can still be shy, my confidence has improved. I now enjoy being around people more than I did before.

Key Tips for Modelling

How to Overcome Stage Fright

Practising regularly and preparing before a show can help build confidence and reduce stage fright.

Posture Correction and Etiquette

Maintain good posture while walking and posing. Keep your back straight and your movements controlled.

Building Confidence and Catwalk Skills

Posing

Learn your best angles and practise your expressions in front of a mirror.

Use props and walls to create more dynamic poses by changing the angles of your limbs.

Walking

Practise walking in heels or flats. Maintain your posture and learn to walk confidently to the music.

If a heel comes off, remain balanced and composed. The show must go on!
